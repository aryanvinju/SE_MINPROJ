How levels are assigned

ML / DL score: Low below 0.30, Moderate from 0.30 to below 0.70, High from 0.70.

QML margin: Low below -0.50, Moderate from -0.50 to below 0.50, High from 0.50.

These are chosen demonstration bands, not medically validated cutoffs. Levels across models are not directly comparable.

What is QML here?

A four-qubit circuit maps compressed inputs into quantum states. A classical SVM learns from their similarities. The circuit runs on a CPU simulator.

ML and DL use all 15 features. QML uses four PCA components. No quantum speed advantage is claimed.
