How scores and levels work

ML and DL use class YES model scores. Low is below 0.30, Moderate is 0.30 to below 0.70, and High starts at 0.70.

QML uses an SVM decision margin, not a probability. Low is below -0.50, Moderate is -0.50 to below 0.50, and High starts at 0.50.

These bands are illustrative and are not medically validated. Levels across models are not directly comparable.

About the quantum model

A four-qubit circuit maps four PCA components into quantum states on a CPU simulator. A classical SVM learns from their similarities. ML and DL use all 15 features. No quantum speed advantage is claimed.
